Personalised Understanding of Learning & Student Experience

Learning
Made Visible.

Learning happens in more places than we can see. PULSE@UNSW explores how learning analytics, AI and human-centred technologies can make learning processes visible — helping students understand their learning and educators design better learning experiences.

Our idea

Learning leaves a PULSE.

Every question, idea, mistake, interaction, piece of feedback and change in approach tells part of the story of learning. We study these signals and turn them into meaningful insights that learners and educators can act on.

Learning Analytics

Making learning processes visible through data, digital traces and meaningful visualisation.

AI & Learning

Understanding how students and educators learn, create and make decisions with AI.

Assessment & Feedback

Rethinking evidence of learning, authentic assessment and feedback in an AI-enabled world.

Metacognition & Student Experience

Helping learners understand, reflect on and regulate their own learning.

Human-Centred EdTech

Designing educational technologies around people, learning and experience.

Computing Education

Investigating how students learn computing and software engineering in rapidly changing environments.
